# Engagement & Social Features

### 1. Mentions (@username)
Allow mentioning users in posts and comments with autocomplete, triggering a notification.

### 2. Share/Repost
Let users share posts to their own feed with an optional quote, similar to a retweet.

### 3. Reading Lists (Public Bookmarks)
Currently bookmarks are private. Let users create named, public reading lists ("My favorite DevOps articles") that others can follow.

### 4. Polls in Posts
Embed simple polls within blog posts to boost reader interaction.

### 5. Variable Reactions (Clap Counter)
Instead of a single like, let users clap multiple times (like Medium) to signal how much they valued a post.
